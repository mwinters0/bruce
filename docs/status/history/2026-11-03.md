## Review of the `SHA256SUMS` call

- **Affirmed (maintainer):** `SHA256SUMS` keeps listing the latest digest under
  its fixed names. A puller's check may fail within Pages' ten minutes after a
  deploy, and a later run passes; only CI has the stale-cache guarantee. Naming
  the digest by content would not have given a puller CI's guarantee anyway: a
  same-day rerun rewrites a dated digest under its name, and a trim drops one that
  last night's `SHA256SUMS` still lists. Filed as D103's `Rejected` line. The
  spec's "The published directory is the contract" is amended to match.
- **The pull's `DEST` must persist between runs** (`docs/manual/deploy.md`,
  "Delivering the results"). docs.fp.o's build volume persists, and its language
  trees are replaced each run (the spec, "Facts the grilling found").
