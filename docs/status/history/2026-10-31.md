# 2026-10-31

## The severity map ranks, and *none* is no finding

Reviewed the 7.5 entry "A severity map moves dots, not rankings or lists".
Settled in the P7 spec, "Severity: three fixed levels, the site mapping each
kind" (2026-10-31). The facts it rests on, as 7.5 left the code:

- The spec's 2026-10-25 *none* is "no dot and no finding"; 7.5 built the dot
  alone, every list, count and ranking keeping a *none* kind's rows.
- A page's and a repo's counts are by outcome (`report_stats`'
  `broken_url`, `broken_anchor`, `error`, and `through`), and `tally`
  (`report::digest::html`) dots each count by its outcome's level, `through`
  by `doubled_slash`'s. A reason key therefore moves its rows' dots and not
  their page's count: under `doubled_slash = "attention"` the links are amber
  rows counted in a red "broken links" on their page, which ranks as red.
- `pages` and `by_repo` order in SQL by `broken_url + error + through`, then
  `broken_anchor` (`report::digest::sections`).
- Levels never reach the gate; its `fail` list (7.5.1) does.

Routed as **7.5.2**, after 7.5.1 and before 7.6: it changes a decision the
spec records, so it is a slice, and 7.5 shipped the contract it replaces.
7.6's `--config` is how a site sees a map's effect without a crawl. D82 and
D76 describe what is built until 7.5.2 amends them.
