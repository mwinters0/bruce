## The severity map ranks, and *none* is no finding

Reviewed the 7.5 entry "A severity map moves dots, not rankings or lists".
Settled in the P7 spec, "Severity: three fixed levels, the site mapping each
kind" (2026-10-31). The facts it rests on, as 7.5 left the code:

- The spec's 2026-10-25 *none* is "no dot and no finding"; 7.5 built the dot
  alone, every list, count and ranking keeping a *none* kind's rows.
- A page's and a repo's counts are by outcome (`report_stats`'
  `broken_url`, `broken_anchor`, `error`, and `through`), and `tally`
  (`report::digest::html`) dots each count by its outcome's level, `through`
  by `doubled_slash`'s. A reason key therefore moves its rows' dots and not
  their page's count: under `doubled_slash = "attention"` the links are amber
  rows counted in a red "broken links" on their page, which ranks as red.
- `pages` and `by_repo` order in SQL by `broken_url + error + through`, then
  `broken_anchor` (`report::digest::sections`).
- Levels never reach the gate; its `fail` list (7.5.1) does.

Routed as **7.5.2**, after 7.5.1 and before 7.6: it changes a decision the
spec records, so it is a slice, and 7.5 shipped the contract it replaces.
7.6's `--config` is how a site sees a map's effect without a crawl. D82 and
D76 describe what is built until 7.5.2 amends them.

## The gate lists what its `fail` list leaves out, and the inverse check follows it

Reviewed the 7.5.1 entry "A fail list leaves an outcome out of the gate's
output, and cannot be empty". Settled in the P7 spec, "What becomes a
setting", item 7 (2026-10-31). The facts it rests on, as 7.5.1 left the code:

- An introduced link whose outcome `gate.fail` leaves out is in no gate
  section: `broken(…, true)` and `broken(…, false)` both filter by the list
  (`report::gate`).
- The entry's reason, that `report broken` and the digest list it, holds for
  `report broken --run N` alone: the digest refuses a `check-dir` run
  (`docs/manual/digest.md`), and a CI job's database is the runner's.
- The gate already lists what never fails: the introduced redirected links
  (D7), every `error` (D16), and `--inverse warn`'s breakage. Every section
  prints `<title>: none` when empty (`report::gate::section`), so a section
  costs a default site one line.
- An inverse row's links will read `broken_url` (`page`, `file`) or
  `broken_anchor` (`anchor`) once the change deploys
  (`report::inverse`), so a site leaving `broken_anchor` off still failed on
  an anchor removal.
- An empty list is refused at load (`config::policy::gate`, `FailOn::from_raw`),
  D83 rejecting it as a gate that cannot fail; under `--inverse fail` it could
  still fail on an inverse row. Once every finding is listed, a site rolling
  out without it must ignore `check-dir`'s exit status in each repo's job,
  which hides status 2 (the check could not run) as well.

Routed as **7.5.3**, after 7.5.2 and before 7.6: it changes D83 and the
spec's item 7, so it is a slice, and 7.5.1 shipped the contract it replaces.
D83 describes what is built until 7.5.3 amends it.
