## P4.2's redirect hop onto a skipped URL, reviewed

- P4.2 followed a hop onto a skipped URL (`Frontier::hop_plan` turns every
  `Unchecked` into a status request). The maintainer ruled `skip` stops a
  chain at the hop, as robots.txt already does (`End::Blocked` in
  `src/crawl.rs`), so the deployer's "don't ask" and robots.txt's agree.
  It also makes skipping a login page mark every link that redirects to
  it, which login detection (rejected in D50) could not do reliably.
- A redirected sitemap's hop stops there too: nothing asks a URL the
  deployer skipped. P4.3's worked example skips the forge's `/user/login*`
  beside `issues/new`, showing the case.
- That is P4.2.1 in the spec, ahead of P4.3; D50 says what is built until
  it lands.

## `unlisted`'s roots, reviewed

- P4.7 names a root with no sitemap below the text table; JSON omits it.
  Affirmed (D6's `Rejected`).
- `report_roots` matched `runs.roots`, written as configured
  (`Config::root_urls`, `src/crawl.rs`'s `Plan::Page`), against canonical

  says so. **Pickup:** P4.7.1, then P4.8.
