## The P10 wrap: M40 and M41

P10 is wrapped (`266e073`), after its repoint (`229318d`), whose body lists
every correction. The maintainer: the repoint's two small defects are
admitted, not yet fixed. Neither blocks a phase, and neither changes a
decision: each brings the code to what the record already says.

- **M40 (KD36).** `cli::export_baseline::export` guards `--out` against
  the database and never `partial(out)`, which it removes before writing:
  `--db X.partial --out X`, `X` absent, deletes the open database and its
  `.wal`. The module's "it is never the database itself" states the intent.
- **M41 (KD37).** `texting_robots` parses a delay with `str::parse::<f32>`,
  so one past `f32::MAX` is infinite (its own
  `test_robot_excessive_crawl_delay`). `fetch::http::host::interval`
  ignores a delay that is not finite, where `MAX_INTERVAL` caps a finite
  one at an hour, and `Host::access` keeps the largest among a name's
  origins, so the infinite one replaces another origin's finite delay. D18
  and D69 (the slowest delay paces the host) and `MAX_INTERVAL`'s rustdoc
  state the intent.
