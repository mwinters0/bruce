# 2026-10-29

## M22: the digest's guide, and its text's future

The maintainer's FAQ for Fedora's beta testers is now the HTML's last
section, "How to read this report" (`report/digest/html/guide.rs`), an ⓘ
beside each term linking to its entry. Two changes to the FAQ as written:

- **Which label is which.** The FAQ had "broken through it" for a slash
  doubled mid-path and "doubled slash" for one at the end. D71 labels by
  role, not position: the linking page's link is `broken_url`, reason
  `doubled_slash`, wherever the `//` falls, and the relative links that
  break on the doubled page are counted on the linking page as "broken
  through it". The 2026-10-28 crawl's digest
  (`runs/en-us-20261028-1452/digest/`) shows both on
  `en-US/project/`: two `doubled_slash` links, one landing on
  `code-of-conduct//` (a slash at the end), 554 broken through it. The
  entry also names a redirect landing on a doubled URL, that page's case.
- **Site-neutral.** `docs.fp.o` is `example.com`, a first-person sentence is
  the report's, and "currently" is dropped.

**P7 owns the guide's text** (maintainer). It states today's judging, which
P7.3 and P7.5 make per-site, so its entries follow the run's policy from
those slices on: `../../wip/roadmap-P7-rules.md`, "The guide follows the
policy".

**Overrides are P11**, sketched in `../../design/roadmap.md`: prose is
presentation, not the policy P7 is about, and a format designed before
the texts to override are known would be redesigned at the first
template.
