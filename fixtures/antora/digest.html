Pages, by the broken links on them

The pages holding the most broken links first, those to a broken URL or that errored (red) before those to a missing fragment (amber), a link counted on the page it is written on. Open one for its links: Written as is the link in the page, Resolves to the URL it names, Kind the element holding it, anchor being an <a href>, and Problem and Status what the target answered.

A link to a URL with a doubled slash in its path is broken whatever the URL answered, reason doubled_slash: the page linking there is the one to correct. The doubled slash is marked, and where a redirect lands on it, the landing is under Resolves to. Its row also counts the links broken through it, the relative links on the pages such a link reaches, which the doubled slash breaks, and ranks by them; those pages count only their absolute links, or none where a page at the URL without the doubled slash was checked.

A link to a URL that answered 404 where the same URL with its final / added or dropped answers is broken all the same, reason wrong_trailing_slash, and Resolves to names that URL to link to instead.
